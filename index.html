Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Flowpath is a free puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices. It applies to Flowpath on both Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

Flowpath itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect certain data as described below.

Data collected automatically by third-party SDKs:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, device model, OS version)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, conversions)
  • App usage and performance diagnostics

2. Google AdMob

Flowpath uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process data as described in Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob-served ads

3. Game Progress Data

Your game progress is stored locally on your device only.

4. Advertising Identifiers

We use device advertising identifiers solely to serve and measure ads — the Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android, and Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS. On iOS, the app requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before accessing the IDFA, in accordance with Apple's guidelines.

5. Children's Privacy

Not directed to children under 13.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data.

7. Data Security

Data is stored locally on your device.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Opt out of ads personalization in device settings (Android: Settings → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking)
  • Reset your Advertising ID / IDFA at any time in device settings
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app

9. Third-Party Links

We are not responsible for third-party sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy anytime.
